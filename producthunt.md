## 详细描述 (Description)

Social Cover Lab generates professional thumbnails & covers for ALL social platforms — RedNote/Xiaohongshu, TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram — in seconds. Upload one photo, type your title, choose a style, and AI handles the rest.

🎯 Why we built this:
We loved xhs-cover (an open-source RedNote cover tool by Vivi) but wanted a tool that works across all platforms, not just one. So we built it — and made it zero-config so anyone can use it instantly.

✨ What makes us different:
• 📱 4 platforms in one tool — RedNote, TikTok, YouTube, Instagram
• 🎨 37 styles across platforms — each optimized for its native format
• 🔌 Zero setup — no API key, no credit card, just upload and go
• 📐 Auto aspect ratio — 3:4, 9:16, 16:9, 1:1 handled automatically
• 🎓 Style learning — upload a reference image, AI extracts and creates matching styles
• 💎 4K resolution on Pro
• 🏢 Agency plan with API access for teams

💡 How it works:
1. Upload your portrait photo
2. Type your title
3. Choose a style & platform
4. AI generates your cover in ~10 seconds

💰 Pricing:
• Free: 1 platform (RedNote), 8 styles, 10/day
• Pro: $12/mo — all platforms, 37 styles, unlimited, 4K
• Agency: $49/mo — API access, 5 seats, batch generation

🛠 Tech stack:
Gemini 3 Pro Image (Nano Banana Pro) under the hood. Built as an EasyClaw skill + web app. MIT licensed.

🙏 Maker's note:
We're creators who spent way too many hours making covers for different platforms. xhs-cover showed us what's possible for one platform — we asked "why not all of them?" This is our answer. Would love your feedback!
